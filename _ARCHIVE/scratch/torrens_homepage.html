Why study with us?

At Torrens University, employability drives everything you study. You will learn from industry experts, build experience through placements and benefit from flexible study options and personalised support that prepares you to take the next step in your career.

#1 in Australia for Skills Development

#1 in Australia for Skills Development, with an outstanding 87% satisfaction, 2 percentage points above last year, 5 above national average,  and the highest score to date. (QILT).*

1:1 guidance from industry experts

Learn from passionate experts who offer industry insights and work-ready skills.*

Graduate work-ready

Our courses are co-designed with industry and taught using the latest technology, ensuring you graduate work-ready.*

Learn from award-winning lecturers

Our MBA lecturers were recognised as the Most Inspirational MBA Lecturers in 2023 and 2024 by MBAus, the professional body for MBAs in Australasia.*

*Sources
