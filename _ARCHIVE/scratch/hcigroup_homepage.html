Institute of Health and Nursing Australia.

We are the Institute of Health and Nursing Australia (IHNA), a healthcare training provider born out of the desire to create jobs and improve people’s lives. Since 2007 we’ve been dedicated to giving the best learning experience to over 22,000 students for a satisfying career in nursing, health, and community services. Our campuses in Melbourne, Sydney, Perth and Brisbane are committed to the delivery of career-focused learning that leads to real-world outcomes.

Quality learning with industry-focused programs.

IHNA is a member of ACPET (Australian Council for Private Education and Training) and is an Australian Registered Training Organisation (RTO), approved by the Australian Standards and Quality Agency (ASQA) to provide education and training courses that prepare students for a global healthcare career. As one of Australia’s leading providers of comprehensive health and nursing education, IHNA’s high quality healthcare courses open a multitude of education pathways and are complemented by continuing professional education and up-skilling programs.

Institute of Health and Management

Since its establishment in 2012, the Institute of Health & Management (IHM) has emerged as a premier provider of exceptional healthcare and nursing education in Australia. Our institute offers a comprehensive range of programs designed for nurses, medical practitioners, dentists, and other healthcare professionals. Courses are available in online and classroom-based delivery modes and are created by experienced nursing academics and clinicians with extensive experience in online teaching and learning.

Empowering healthcare professionals.

With a commitment to excellence, IHM has obtained recognition as an institute of higher education by the Australian Government's Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Agency (TEQSA). This accreditation ensures that our programs meet rigorous standards and provide students with the knowledge and skills needed to excel in their chosen healthcare careers.

MWT Australia

At MWT Australia, we believe that career-focussed education and global opportunities are the way to transform your future. We are a one-stop centre for overseas education, international student recruitment, employment guidance, placement and settlement services.

MWT Australia comprises a group of forward-thinking companies that offer work-ready education and systems to advance the careers of young professionals in the country and abroad, primarily in the field of healthcare. We leverage the latest technologies and collaborative tools in the design and delivery of our support services, which include consultancy, education and training, placements services.

Infection Control Training in the Australian Healthcare Setting

Infection control is a key component of healthcare, protecting the safety of patients and healthcare staff by avoiding the spread of infectious diseases. As the frontline warriors in the battle against diseases, nurses must learn to reduce and prevent the spread of infections. In the Australian healthcare context, rigorous infection control training is mandatory for all healthcare workers, which is driven by stringent laws and a dedication to high standards of care.
