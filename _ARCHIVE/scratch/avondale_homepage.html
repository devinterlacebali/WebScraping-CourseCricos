Lake Macquarie

Avondale's picturesque main campus is located only 45 minutes from Newcastle and 90 minutes from Sydney on the Avondale Estate in Cooranbong, NSW.

Sydney Campus

The Sydney Campus is home to the Faculty of Nursing and Health and is located on the grounds of the Sydney Adventist Hospital, the largest private hospital in New South Wales.

Study Online

Many of our courses are also offered online with great academic and student support for flexible learning.

Nursing

Choose Australia's #1 undergraduate nursing degree* offered at both Sydney and Lake Macquarie campuses.

Teaching

Specialise in primary, secondary, early childhood and more with a range of postgraduate options. Our undergraduate teaching degrees are rated #1 for overall educational experience.*

Arts

Explore your unique interests and develop versatile skills in problem solving, communication, creativity and critical thinking.

Business

Major in accounting, marketing and human resource management with work placements opportunities, and access to industry experts.

Counselling

Become a mental health professional by developing skills in counselling, working with children and adolescents and understanding families and couple relationships.

Ministry & Theology

Develop the skills needed to build a strong church community and work in a range of church and not-for-profit organisations.

Lifestyle Medicine

Empower and enhance existing health care with innovative lifestyle-based therapies.

Outdoor Leadership

Professional training in an exciting array of challenging adventure activities
