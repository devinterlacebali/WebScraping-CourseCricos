Summary

In today’s fast-moving and complex business environment, those with advanced technical skills in accounting, analytics, economics, finance, information systems and marketing insights will find themselves in demand in every area of business. A knowledge of commerce is also increasingly valuable for careers in fields such as health, law, psychology, science and technology.

The Bachelor of Commerce focuses on data-driven decision-making, which, when combined with interdisciplinary learning and extensive industry engagement, will set you apart when you graduate. Your competitive edge is further enhanced via an embedded employability unit – learn to craft a standout CV and LinkedIn profile, refine your personal brand and sharpen your interview skills – and tailored industry events that facilitate networking opportunities. And you’ll become adept in key competencies highly regarded by today’s top employers: data literacy, business analysis, teamwork, communication, conflict resolution, problem-solving and critical thinking – all with a global mindset.

Academic requirements

International selection rank: 80

The selection rank indicates the minimum academic requirement for entry to this course and is based on the Australian Tertiary Admission Rank (ATAR) system.

How is the international selection rank calculated?

If you completed high school outside Australia, your overseas qualification results will be converted into an Australian selection rank. You can use our international qualification converter tool to understand the minimum score required under your qualification’s grading scheme.

Which qualifications are recognised?

Macquarie recognises qualifications from more than 70 countries for direct entry to our courses. These include:

  • senior secondary qualifications
  • foundation programs
  • associate degrees, bachelor degrees and diplomas assessed as comparable to Australian qualifications.

What if I don’t meet the academic requirements?

If you don’t qualify for direct entry, you may be considered for a packaged offer that includes a suitable pathway program through the Macquarie University College. If you’ve completed an Australian Year 12 qualification (NSW HSC or interstate equivalent) or the International Baccalaureate (IB) in Australia and don’t meet the ATAR requirement, you may still be considered for admission under our Subject Advantage Entry Scheme, which considers your overall performance and results in specific subjects relevant to your chosen degree. If eligible, you’ll be automatically considered when applying through UAC.

Entry pathways into Macquarie

The Macquarie University College offers three pathways designed to help you meet our undergraduate entry requirements: the Standard Foundation Program, the Intensive Program and diplomas. Once you complete your pathway program and meet the entry requirements for your chosen degree, you can transition into either the first or second year.

English language requirements

All Macquarie University applicants are required to provide evidence of English language proficiency. The standard of English required for entry is based on the IELTS Academic test. Other accepted evidence of English proficiency, including minimum scores on other English tests and qualifications, can be checked using the conversion tool.

The required standard of English for entry is:

The required standard of English
IELTS bandMinimum score required
Overall6.5
Reading6
Writing6
Listening6
Speaking6

You may also satisfy the English language requirements by:

  • passing an approved English language test at the required level
  • holding a qualification from an approved country with English as the language of instruction
  • completing an approved high school qualification or foundation program with study in English
  • completing an approved English for Academic Purposes (EAP) program.

What if I don't meet the requirements?

If you don’t meet the English requirements for your course, you may be issued an offer of study that includes an English preparation program at the Macquarie University English Language Centre (ELC). If you receive a packaged offer, you can apply for a single student visa covering both your English preparation program and your course.

The length of your English program will depend on your current English level and the minimum English requirements for your course. Our admissions team will be able to recommend the number of weeks required when you submit your application.

What English language tests are approved?

We accept the IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT (internet-based), Pearson Test of English (PTE) Academic, Cambridge English Qualifications (C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency), and the Occupational English Test (OET).
